About Notes

A personal collection of study notes on computer science, algorithms, and artificial intelligence.

What these notes are for

These notes support my own learning and revision. They bring together concepts, formulas, examples, and code from courses and other learning materials, with an emphasis on explanations that can be read independently. Coverage and depth vary by subject, and the notes are revised as my understanding develops.

Using the notes
